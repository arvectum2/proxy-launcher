Connect your iPhone through a proxy you configure, with clear controls and flexible routing.

• Multiple profiles, priorities and automatic backup failover
• Website exclusions for destinations that should connect directly
• Open/close automations for selected apps using iOS Shortcuts
• Proxy settings remain on your device; passwords are kept in Keychain
