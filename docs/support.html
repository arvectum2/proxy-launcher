Arvectum Proxy Launcher — Support

APL is a local utility for connecting an iPhone through a proxy supplied by the user.

Before contacting support

Check that the proxy host, port, type, username and password are correct, and that the proxy accepts connections from the network used by the iPhone. Connection diagnostics are available from the in-app Journal.
