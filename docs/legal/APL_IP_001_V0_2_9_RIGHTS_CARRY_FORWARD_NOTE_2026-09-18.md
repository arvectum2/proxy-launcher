# APL-IP-001 — v0.2.9 rights carry-forward note

Status: **REVIEW NOTE / NOT A LEGAL OPINION / HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED**  
Date: 2026-09-18  
Tracking: #57

## Purpose

This note explains why moving APL-IP-001 from historical v0.2.5 to current v0.2.9 does not automatically require executing a duplicate version-number-specific transfer, while also avoiding the opposite unsupported assumption that every later creative contribution is automatically covered.

## Existing evidence

The repository records an executed private 2026-09-14 sole-participant/rightsholder decision concerning **Arvectum Proxy Launcher** and contribution of the exclusive right to ООО «Арвектум». The public receipt intentionally omits the private document text and personal/signature data.

The existing instrument remains the primary rights-chain evidence and should not be replaced merely because the public release number advanced from 0.2.5 to 0.2.9.

## Current Russian-law baseline used for the review packet

- Civil Code Article 1234 governs transfer of an exclusive right in full.
- Article 1285 applies that model to works.
- Supreme Court Plenum No. 10 of 23.04.2019, paragraph 47, states that an agreement may provide for transfer/licensing of a result that will be created in the future, provided the object is sufficiently individualized when the right is transferred.
- The same Plenum explains that modification of a computer program can create a derivative work and that the right to modify is within the exclusive right of the rightsholder.
- Where a later contribution is a service work, Article 1295 governs the employer/author allocation subject to its conditions.

These rules mean that the **exact wording and factual creation basis matter**. The absence of a version number is relevant but is not, by itself, proof that all future creative contributions were disposed of.

## Operational conclusion for APL-IP-001

1. Do **not** prepare a new transfer solely because the release number changed.
2. Preserve the executed 2026-09-14 decision as the base chain-of-title instrument.
3. For v0.2.9, inspect the exact private wording and identify the rights basis for material contributions made after 2026-09-14.
4. If the existing instrument sufficiently covers future results/modifications, record that finding and evidence; no duplicate version-specific transfer is needed for APL-IP-001.
5. If it does not, use the actual applicable basis (for example service-work allocation or a separate assignment) rather than inventing a repository conclusion.
6. Keep R-1B fail-closed until an authorized human makes that factual/legal determination.

## Repository boundary

This note does not sign, amend, replace or interpret the private instrument conclusively. It exists to prevent two errors:

- needless repeated assignment for every software version; and
- unsupported carry-forward of rights to later creative contributions.
