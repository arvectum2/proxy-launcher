## Proposed private agreement terms

Parties:

- Acquirer: ООО «Арвектум», represented by [AUTHORIZED SIGNATORY / BASIS]

1. The agreement supplements and does not cancel the executed 2026-09-14 instrument concerning Arvectum Proxy Launcher.

2. Existing post-2026-09-14 results. The Author transfers to ООО «Арвектум» in full the exclusive right, to the extent it arose in the Author, in human-authored protected changes incorporated after v0.2.5 through exact v0.2.9.

Technical identification of the current object:

3. Future results. The Author agrees to transfer to ООО «Арвектум» in full the exclusive right in protected results intentionally created by the Author as part of development of the product Arvectum Proxy Launcher after the agreement date.

4. Future-result individualization. A future result is within scope when it is:
- intentionally created by the Author as part of Arvectum Proxy Launcher;
- documented by a development task, source/repository history, release record or other technical evidence linking it to that product.

5. Platform neutrality. The future-rights clause is not limited by current version number, operating system, package type or distribution form. It may cover future Arvectum Proxy Launcher implementations for Windows, Linux, macOS, AppImage, Android, iOS and other platforms when such protected results are created by the Author as part of the same product.

6. Transfer timing.
- Rights in already-created post-2026-09-14 results transfer at execution of the agreement.
- Rights in a future result transfer at the moment the exclusive right in that result arises in the Author, but not earlier.

7. Scope. The exclusive right is transferred in full for the entire term and without territorial limitation. Personal non-property rights are not transferred.

8. Consideration. The parties must expressly state whether the transfer is gratuitous or remunerated. The intended current structure is GRATUITOUS, subject to final human/legal review before signature.

9. Third-party boundary. The agreement transfers only rights actually belonging to the Author. It does not purport to transfer third-party OSS or other third-party rights governed by their own licenses.

10. AI-assisted development. The agreement may record that AI systems were used as development tools under human architecture, selection, review, correction and final acceptance, consistent with the existing APL-IP-001 factual record.

11. No per-version re-signing. If the future-results clause is validly executed and sufficiently identifies future Arvectum Proxy Launcher results, a new rights-assignment instrument is not intended merely because a new version, AppImage build, Android app, iOS app or other platform implementation is later added. Each such platform/release still requires its own technical, licensing, security and release/compliance approval.

12. Execution discipline.
- The executed agreement is private and must not be committed to the public repository.
- After signature, record only a safe receipt, signing date, storage reference and SHA-256 digest.
- R-1B remains OPEN until actual execution is confirmed.
- Final APL-IP-001 APPROVED / REMEDIATION REQUIRED remains a separate authorized human decision.

## Legal basis used for drafting

This draft is structured around the current Russian-law baseline that:
- an exclusive right may be transferred in full by written agreement;
- a contract may provide for transfer of rights in a result that will arise in the future, with transfer no earlier than the moment the right arises;
- the object must be sufficiently individualized;
- the present company structure is a single-participant company whose sole participant is also its sole executive body, but that factual status must still be true on the signing date.

This is a drafting aid, not a legal opinion.
