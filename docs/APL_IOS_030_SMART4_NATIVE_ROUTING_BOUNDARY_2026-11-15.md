# Smart Routing 4.0 — native data-plane boundary (15 November 2026)

Scope: APL iOS 0.3.0 unified candidate, not a change to the active 0.2.0 release. Decision based on inspected exact sources, not a successful device test.

## Concrete incompatibility fixed

The selected loopback-clean tun2proxy archive originates from e271de19683937f23d3f8f0eb4df0a61fc4a6e50 plus `Tools/NativeTunnelLab/tun2proxy-connected-udp-loopback-clean.patch`. Its `src/args.rs` has virtual DNS and IP/CIDR bypass arguments but not the older APL split-DNS and experimental scoped-direct arguments. `XraySocksHandoffPlan` previously accepted domains/IPs and constructed bare `--dns-bypass HOST` arguments, whereas even the older legacy patch requires HOST=IP. Tests cover domain, IPv4/IPv6, HOST=IP, duplicate/oversize and injected arguments. The clean candidate remains isolated and synthetic only.

## Engineering direction and constraints

Apple TN3120 (official developer technote, revised 22 July 2025, checked 15 November 2026): https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3120-expected-use-cases-for-network-extension-packet-tunnel-providers . It describes full tunnel to a remote gateway and selective destination-IP tunneling as expected use cases. It explicitly identifies selective alternate-interface forwarding of already captured traffic, broad system DNS interception and hosting a proxy listener inside PacketTunnel as unsupported scenarios. This is platform guidance, not a determination of legality or guarantee of App Review. The embedded local SOCKS handoff itself also needs platform review; passing a compile or LAN test does not resolve that question. No production enablement of the historical scoped-direct experiment is authorized.

- Native tunnel-only is the present bounded candidate. For full-tunnel WireGuard/AWG, direct/proxy policy at an owned remote gateway could be a separate server-side product feature; traffic that exits remotely is not local DIRECT and cannot be advertised as Russian/local egress. Do not modify 0.3.0 scope/delete exclusions without owner decision.
- Destination-IP selective tunneling is a different capability: explicit IP/prefix routes can be considered separately, with provider-owned peer/underlay sockets independently proven, IPv4/IPv6 and DNS consistency, and no implicit endpoint-wide or hostname-resolved IP exclusions. Shared CDN addresses cannot establish exact-host permission. Include/exclude semantics and DNS leakage must be reviewed and tested on device, never assume current default routes implement this.
- Per-app VPN is distinct from arbitrary consumer app exclusions. Verify deployment/management entitlement and distribution constraints before planning an enterprise mode. On macOS evaluate NETransparentProxyProvider separately; do not infer iOS support.
- Existing SmartFlowRoutingPolicy and Smart4OriginalFlowTrace preserve exact-host approval and provenance concepts. Neither DNS match, raw IP, sniffed name nor engine startup alone proves correct external egress. A supported design must bind policy to original session and network generation, independently verify TCP/UDP/QUIC and both IP families, reject unknown/expired/mismatched names, and drain/revoke safely on Wi-Fi/LTE transitions. The existing physically scoped DIRECT experiment remains research only; do not silently resurrect it via Xray freedom, Rust no-proxy, or AWG socket binding.
